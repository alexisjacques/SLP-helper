Tip: The numbered questions are optional. Use them as a conversational guide to gather enough information to choose a rating for each section.

Say / Ask Large bold text is read aloud to the resident. Smaller boxed text is for the administrator only.

Intro

Social, introductory cues to start a conversation with the resident.
1

Say“Hi ____, how are you today?” (extend your hand and shake the resident’s hand)

2

Say“My name is ____. I came by to visit with you.”

3

Say“I just wanted to stop by to visit and see how you’re doing.”

4

Ask“How has your day been?”

5

Ask“Did you have a good (breakfast, lunch, dinner)?”

Past Memory

6

Ask“I was born in ____ (city and state), where were you born?”

7

Ask“I work in health care, what kind of work did you do?”

8

Ask“I went to ____ (school) and on to college, how about you?”

9

Ask“Do you remember the name of your teachers or classmates?”

Recent Memory

Pertains to past memory; use a picture of a school and teacher to see if any type of response emerges from visual cues.
10

Ask“I was just watching the news, do you know the name of the President of the United States?”

If no response, give a choice of a few, such as Biden, Trump, Obama, or Bush.
11

Ask“I live in ____ (city and state), where do you live?”

Some residents will respond, “Right here.”
12

Ask“What were you doing just before I came in to visit you?”

Concentration

The object of this task is to judge the resident’s ability to concentrate (attention span), not just their ability to count.
20
If the resident appears very impaired from previous responses:

Say“I want to play a counting game with you. Let’s count from 1 to 10.”

If the resident has difficulty, start them by saying “one” and holding up one finger. If they make it to 10:

Say“Now let’s count down from 10 to 1, like they do with a rocket ship.”

21
If the resident does well counting backwards from 10 to 1:

Say“You are really good at this, let’s try something else.”

Proceed to counting from 30 backwards by 2’s, or saying the months of the year backwards.

Global Deterioration Scale

GDS stage Total score Interpretation
1 5–7 No cognitive decline (normal)
2 8–12 Very mild cognitive decline (age associated memory impairment)
3 13–17 Mild cognitive decline (mild cognitive impairment)
4 18–22 Moderate cognitive decline (mild dementia)
5 23–27 Moderately severe cognitive decline (moderate dementia)
6 28–32 Severe cognitive decline (moderately severe dementia)
7 33–35 Very severe cognitive decline (severe or late dementia)

GDS stage = total score ÷ 5, rounded to the nearest whole number.
