Brief Cognitive Rating Scale. Open the form (PDF)

Tip: The numbered questions are optional. Use them as a conversational guide to gather enough information to choose a rating for each section.

Intro

Intended to be social, introductory cues to initiate conversation with the resident.

  1. “Hi __________ how are you today?” (extend your hand and shake the resident’s hand).
  2. “My name is _________. I came by to visit with you.”
  3. “I just wanted to stop by to visit and see how you’re doing.”
  4. “How has your day been?”
  5. “Did you have a good (breakfast, lunch, dinner)?”

Past Memory

  1. “I was born in __________ (city and state), where were you born?”
  2. “I work in health care, what kind of work did you do?”
  3. “I went to ___________ (school) and on to college, how about you?”
  4. “Do you remember the name of your teachers or classmates?”

Rating

Recent Memory

Impairment of Recent Memory. Pertain to past memory; use a picture of a school and teacher to see if any type of response emerges from visual cues.

  1. “I was just watching the news, do you know the name of the President of the United States?” (If no response, give a choice of a few such as Biden, Trump, Obama, or Bush).
  2. “I live in ___________ (city and state), where do you live?” (Note: Some residents will respond, “Right here”.)
  3. “What were you doing just before I came in to visit you?”

Rating

Orientation

*Note – If the resident does not know who the president is or where they live, additional specific orientation questions may only frustrate the resident.

  1. “What city are we in now?”
  2. “What was your name again?”
  3. “Do you know what month it is?”
  4. “Do you know what year it is?”
  5. “Do you know what day of the week it is?”
  6. “Do you know what time it is, approximately?”
  7. “Are you married? What is your spouse’s name?”

Rating

Concentration

Object of this task is to judge the resident’s ability to concentrate (attention span) and not just ability to count with numbers.

  1. Judging from previous responses, if the resident appears to be very impaired, say “I want to play a counting game with you. Let’s count from 1-10.” If the resident has difficulty, try starting them by saying “one” and holding up one finger. If the resident makes it to 10, say “Now let’s count down from 10 to 1, like they do with a rocket ship.”
  2. If the resident does well counting backwards from 10-1, say “You are really good at this, let’s try something else.” Proceed to counting from 30 backwards by 2’s or saying the months of the year backwards.
