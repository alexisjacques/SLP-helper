Block design

Say“You see these blocks, they are all alike. On some sides they are all red, on some all white, and on some half red and half white. I am going to put the blocks together to make them look like this picture. Watch me first. Now, look at the picture and make one just like it with the blocks.”

Demonstrate the practice design slowly, mix the blocks, and have the patient do it. If they fail in 90 seconds, mix the blocks and let them try again; if they fail again, go on. Mix the blocks after each design. After the practice item there is no demonstration or second attempt.
