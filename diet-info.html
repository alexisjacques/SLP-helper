FOIS

Functional Oral Intake Scale

  1. 1Nothing by mouth
  2. 2Tube dependent with minimal attempts of food or liquid
  3. 3Tube dependent with consistent oral intake of food or liquid
  4. 4Total oral diet of a single consistency
  5. 5Total oral diet with multiple consistencies, but requiring special preparation or compensations
  6. 6Total oral diet with multiple consistencies without special preparation, but with specific food limitations
  7. 7Total oral diet with no restrictions

Crary, Mann & Groher (2005)

PAS

Penetration-Aspiration Scale

No entry Penetration (2–5) Aspiration (6–8)
  1. 1Material does not enter the airway
  2. 2Material enters the airway, remains above the vocal folds, and is ejected from the airway
  3. 3Material enters the airway, remains above the vocal folds, and is not ejected from the airway
  4. 4Material enters the airway, contacts the vocal folds, and is ejected from the airway
  5. 5Material enters the airway, contacts the vocal folds, and is not ejected from the airway
  6. 6Material enters the airway, passes below the vocal folds, and is ejected into the larynx or out of the airway
  7. 7Material enters the airway, passes below the vocal folds, and is not ejected from the trachea despite effort
  8. 8Material enters the airway, passes below the vocal folds, and no effort is made to eject

Rosenbek et al. (1996)
