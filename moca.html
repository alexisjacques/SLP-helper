8

Say“Now, I will ask you to count by subtracting 7 from 100, and then, keep subtracting 7 from your answer until I tell you to stop.”

Each subtraction is scored independently: if the patient gives a wrong number but correctly subtracts 7 from it afterwards, count those later answers as correct.
The patient must do this in their head, without fingers or paper and pencil. Do not repeat the patient’s answers. If they ask for their last answer or what number to subtract, repeat the instructions if you have not already.
Scoring: 4–5 correct = 3 pts, 2–3 correct = 2 pts, 1 correct = 1 pt, 0 correct = 0 pts.
